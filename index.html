Stack Counter

Brochures and magazines, one stack at a time.

  1. Square-on. Hold the phone level with the side of the stack, not looking down at it.
  2. Fill the frame along the stack, from the bottom item to the top item.
  3. Light from the side, or turn the flash on, so every edge casts a line.

Tap the middle of the first item.

-

    Each tick is one detected edge. Gaps or doubled ticks show where to look.
